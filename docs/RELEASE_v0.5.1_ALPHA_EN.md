# TLFL v0.5.1-alpha — Interface Foundations

`v0.5.1-alpha` expands the compact `v0.5.0-alpha` surface into a modular formal
map of the current research program.

- independent `RawOccurs` and an explicit `PlanckTouchBridge`;
- the conditional result `Something /\ Not Nothing /\ Not Collapsed`;
- a local questioner without an automatic external-questioner inference;
- a two-sided interface with reverse and forward channels;
- a counterexample to the existence of a least positive touch;
- two-axis time as a relation between physical and internal records;
- the boundary of universally exact endpoint-only prediction;
- a two-point external trace, retraction, and monicity result;
- a semisimplicial boundary with `d squared = 0`;
- a nearby-goal field with memory, noise, and a preserved boundary.

## Red boundary

questioner, intentional design, or a supra-domain. `PlanckTouchBridge` remains
an explicit obligation. Positive contact alone does not guarantee a least
positive contact.
